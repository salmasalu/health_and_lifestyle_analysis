# Health Lifestyle Analysis

## Overview

This project explores a health and lifestyle dataset containing 100,000 records. The goal of the analysis is to understand how daily habits such as physical activity, sleep, hydration, smoking, alcohol consumption, and diet relate to overall health indicators and disease risk.

Using Python and data visualization techniques, the project identifies patterns, trends, and relationships that can help in understanding healthy lifestyle behaviors.

## Objectives

* Explore health and lifestyle patterns in the dataset.
* Analyze relationships between lifestyle habits and health metrics.
* Study factors associated with disease risk.
* Compare health indicators across different age groups and genders.
* Generate meaningful insights from the data.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

* Age
* Gender
* BMI
* Daily Steps
* Sleep Hours
* Water Intake
* Calories Consumed
* Smoking Status
* Alcohol Consumption
* Resting Heart Rate
* Blood Pressure
* Cholesterol Levels
* Family History
* Disease Risk

The dataset consists of **100,000 records** and includes both numerical and categorical variables.

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

* Data cleaning and validation
* Missing value and duplicate checks
* Distribution analysis
* Lifestyle habit analysis
* Health metric analysis
* Disease risk analysis
* Correlation analysis
* Outlier detection
* Feature engineering
* Data visualization

## Feature Engineering

To gain deeper insights, several new features were created:

* Efficiency Steps per Calorie
* Hydration per BMI
* Sleep Quality Index
* Blood Pressure Ratio
* Health Stress Index
* Lifestyle Score
* Age Group Classification

## Key Insights

* Physical activity showed a relationship with BMI and overall lifestyle quality.
* Sleep patterns varied across age groups and genders.
* Smoking and alcohol consumption were associated with higher health risks.
* Cholesterol levels and blood pressure showed a positive relationship.
* Family history appeared to influence disease risk.
* Lifestyle habits collectively contributed to overall health outcomes.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights how everyday lifestyle choices can influence health and wellness. Factors such as regular physical activity, proper sleep, healthy eating habits, and adequate hydration play an important role in maintaining overall well-being. The findings from this project provide useful insights into the connection between lifestyle behaviors and health outcomes.
